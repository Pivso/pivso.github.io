Bro-tivator — Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026. Applies to the Bro-tivator app on Google Play (io.github.pivso.brotivator).

Bro-tivator shows you short messages from your bro and, if you allow it, sends a few of them as notifications through the day. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers, and it does not collect personal data. The one thing you can pay for is an optional tip, which goes through Google Play. This page says exactly what the app keeps and where anything goes.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings: how many notifications a day, and the hours they may arrive in.
  • Today's shown messages, so the same one isn't sent twice in a day. Cleared every midnight.

None of this is sent anywhere by Bro-tivator. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

If you have Android's own backup switched on, Android may include these settings in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is made and kept by Google under your account; Bro-tivator has no access to it.

Tips

Settings has a "Support the developer" button that lets you leave a tip as an in-app purchase. It unlocks nothing. The payment is made and kept by Google Play under your Google account, and Bro-tivator never sees your payment details. The app receives only Google Play's confirmation that a tip was paid, which it uses to complete the purchase and keeps nothing of. The developer sees the order in Google Play's sales reports, as for any purchase on Play.

Tips use Google Play's billing library, which Google provides. It talks to Google Play to show prices and take payments, and may send Google diagnostic information about that connection. That is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by Bro-tivator.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the messages you scheduled. Nothing else.
  • Run at startup, wake lock, foreground service, network state: used by Android's WorkManager to deliver scheduled notifications on time, including after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play billing and internet: used only by Google Play's billing library for the tips above. Bro-tivator itself does not contact anything on the internet.

Bro-tivator does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Bro-tivator is not directed at children under 13.
